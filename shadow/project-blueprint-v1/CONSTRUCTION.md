# Construction boundary — deterministic fields before the domain candidate

BLUEPRINT_CONSTRUCTION_V1 is a scoped, opt-in construction policy. It is not SessionGraph or a lesson plan and cannot approve a project. The live semantic reviewer and domain/Gate remain unchanged.

## Historical causes, without rerunning the old generation or review

In revision 2 of round2-generated-positive-1790193176957.json, third grade's official text begins `Indagación acerca` although the snapshot says `Indaga acerca`. The compiler now owns PDA identifiers, grade and exact official text; the source is never rewritten.

Only art_acercamiento, art_integracion and art_terminal were listed as session outputs. Eight intermediate artifacts lacked a producing session: art_identificacion, art_recuperacion, art_planificacion, art_produccion, art_reconocimiento, art_concrecion, art_difusion and art_consideraciones. That broke provenance and the link between stage methodology operations and their producing session. The 11 stage estimates also totaled 375 minutes against 270 available.

## Precomputed plan and scope

The current limited policy supports Proyectos Comunitarios with three sessions (the positive input) and one session (the prepared adversarial). Unsupported combinations fail explicitly rather than use proportional allocation. This is not a general architecture policy for all methods or session counts.

For the three-session case, the official PLANEACION, ACCION and INTERVENCION operation groups map explicitly to planning, production and intervention work packages. Each has one distinct artifact and one producing session. Dependencies follow those purposes. There is no Math.floor, operation-index distribution, artificial time percentage or requirement for 11 stages. Each session has the teacher's full 90-minute ceiling; the global ceiling is 270. Stage estimates remain authored by the model and must be positive integers within the hard limits. They are rejected, never scaled or silently shortened. The single-session adversarial uses one integrated stage with its actual one-minute ceiling; semantic feasibility still must be judged, not inferred from the numeric bound.

The plan includes immutable snapshot-derived PDA/grade bindings, operation-to-stage/session/artifact coverage slots, the artifact DAG and time budgets. Action/evidence slots explicitly require model-authored content: precomputation does not pretend to supply pedagogical proof.

## Authorship and compiler ownership

Gemini authors the problem interpretation, learning contributions, stage purposes, observable actions, artifacts' pedagogical descriptions, evidence, criteria, estimates and session progression prose. Each required methodology operation must have a literal action quote from its assigned stage and a literal evidence quote from its output or acceptance criteria. These checks establish traceability, not semantic truth. The unchanged semantic reviewer remains responsible for whether the quoted action actually performs that operation.

The provider transport schema omits compiler-owned fields. The compiler adds exact PDA grade/text, grade-owned PDA lists, deterministic integration destinations, stage order, artifact IDs/links, session input/output lists, methodology lists and budget metadata. An unknown or missing PDA/grade/stage/session/operation is rejected. Cross-grade PDA claims are rejected. The compiler does not rewrite pedagogy, official content, estimates or the domain contract. Full raw proposals, plan hashes and construction records are preserved separately from the domain candidate. Orchestrator identity/revision fields are assigned as before, and final candidate hashes are then evaluated normally.

A failed construction stops without another generation. Any remaining deterministic Gate failure also stops without using pedagogical repair. Only an evaluable, deterministically valid candidate with a semantic failure may use the existing single repair. Prior schema tests and historical review outputs are not used to fabricate approval.

Test-only synthetic quotes are labeled synthetic and do not count as real pedagogical approvals.

## Contract changes after v4

The real Gemini v4 authoring candidate exposed two remaining classes of weakness: a functional requirement could be proposed in the wrong project stage and semantically constitutive product work could be hidden as `COMPLEMENTARY` while a later wrap-up activity claimed the whole artifact.

The current contract now requires:

- source-scoped authoring preflight for session methodology operations, expected artifacts, PDA/grade focus and functional-requirement `stage_ids`;
- `WORKED` only when the same activity performs the complete cognitive action and purpose of the PDA;
- `EXPLICA + PROPONE` in the same activity when required by the official PDA;
- `INDAGA + compartir importancia` with accessible source/consultation, processing or contrast, traceable record and sharing of significance in the same `WORKED` activity;
- substantive execution for production/construction/intervention/diffusion/evaluation methodology operations;
- `DIRECT` for observable constitutive parts of the session artifact instead of concentrating all direct contribution in a final wrap-up.
